# Brand foundation

Scriptural and lexical roots for ChapelCloud’s identity. Verses cited from KJV (`eng-kjv2006`) via UberBible; used as **operating meaning**, not hero slogans (see ADR-0002 and the business plan).

---

## Name

**ChapelCloud** — one word, capital C on both halves.

| Half | Meaning we carry |
|------|------------------|
| **Chapel** | A holy place or sanctuary (Easton: “A holy place or sanctuary”). The gathered ministry — people, place, and mission we serve. |
| **Cloud** | Hebrew *`anan* (H6051): a cloud as covering. Easton: symbol of Divine presence — splendour concealed so it can be near; guidance (pillar); glory filling the house. Greek *nephos* (G3509) in Hebrews 12:1: the “cloud of witnesses.” |

Domain: **chapel.cloud** · Contact: **hello@chapel.cloud**

## Core images (not marketing copy)

### 1. Covering presence

> Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.  
> — Exodus 40:34

> …the cloud filled the house of the LORD  
> — 1 Kings 8:10

**Brand takeaway:** Good technology should feel like a covering — present, protective, ambient — not a spectacle competing with worship. Cloud here means *nearness and care*, not hype.

### 2. Guidance along the way

> And the LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way; and by night in a pillar of fire, to give them light…  
> — Exodus 13:21

**Brand takeaway:** We lead ministries through technical wilderness with steady guidance (day) and usable light when things fail (night / Sunday). Calm direction over flashy dashboards.

### 3. Quiet witness (light)

> Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.  
> — Matthew 5:16

Greek *phos* (G5457): luminousness — to shine or make manifest.  
**Brand takeaway:** Excellence that points beyond us. Christian identity is **evident**, not shouty. Non-believers should feel respected on chapel.cloud.

### 4. Faithful stewardship

> Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.  
> — 1 Corinthians 4:2

> …as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.  
> — 1 Peter 4:10

Greek *oikonomos* (G3623): house-manager / overseer — the one entrusted with another’s household.  
Nave’s *STEWARD*: the faithful steward must be found faithful.  
**Brand takeaway:** Church data, budgets, and uptime are entrusted gifts. We manage the house; we do not own the mission.

### 5. Honest weights and counted cost

> A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight.  
> — Proverbs 11:1

> …counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?  
> — Luke 14:28

**Brand takeaway:** Market-rate honesty, disclosed custom quotes, lean plans we can finish. No bait-and-switch SaaS theater.

### 6. Work as unto the Lord

> And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men;  
> — Colossians 3:23

**Brand takeaway:** Diligence over hype. Quality that serves people and the Gospel.

## What we are not

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Scripture spam in heroes / ads | Verses bind *us*; they are not bait for clicks |
| Cult aesthetic / performative religion | Quiet witness; welcome seekers and nonprofit partners |
| Generic “cloud” tech clichés (purple neon, rocket ships) | Our cloud is covering and presence, not startup cosplay |
| Competing with worship | Brand stays calm so ministry stays central |

---

## Mission (from business plan)

Help churches and ministries steward technology faithfully so staff and volunteers can focus on people and the Gospel — not broken Wi‑Fi, opaque giving tools, or Sunday-morning panic.

## Vision

Become a trusted name in church technology: cloud tools, remote IT care, security that protects people and property, and (later) purpose-built hardware — managed as one calm covering.
